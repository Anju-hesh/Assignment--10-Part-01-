Fry Vegiteable

carrot,beet,beans,pumking & etc

500/= Up

    Ingredients

  • 5+ Vegiteable (about 1.7 kg)
  • 5cm fresh unpeeled ginger, crushed
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1/2 bunch spring onion greens
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp MSG
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 chicken carcass or 4 chicken wings
  • Extra chicken fat to render (you'll need around 1/2 a cup of rendered fat in total)
  • 1 tsp salt

    Vegetable Fry Recipe & Steps

  1. Wash and peel all the vegetables you plan to use, such as carrots, beans, bell peppers, cabbage, and potatoes.
  2. Cut the vegetables into thin, even-sized strips or bite-sized pieces for uniform cooking.
  3. Heat oil in a large frying pan or wok over medium heat.
  4. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter to release their aroma.
  5. Add curry leaves and finely chopped green chilies to the hot oil, sauté for a few seconds.
  6. Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn translucent.
  7. Add minced garlic and ginger, stir-fry for another minute until fragrant.
  8. Toss in the harder vegetables first like carrots and beans. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
  9. Add softer vegetables like bell peppers, cabbage, and potatoes. Continue stir-frying.
  10. Sprinkle turmeric powder, chili powder, salt, and a pinch of black pepper. Mix well.
  11. Add a splash of water if needed to help the vegetables cook without burning, cover and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  12. Once vegetables are tender but still slightly crisp, remove the lid and stir-fry on high heat for 2-3 minutes to evaporate any excess moisture.
  13. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves.
  14. Serve hot as a side dish with rice, roti, or bread.
