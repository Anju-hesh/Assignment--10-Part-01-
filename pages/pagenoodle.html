    Sri Lankan Noodles Recipe & Steps

  1. Boil noodles according to package instructions until just tender, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add sliced onions, garlic, and green chilies. Stir-fry until onions soften.
  4. Add diced vegetables like carrots, cabbage, and bell peppers. Stir-fry for a few minutes.
  5. Add cooked chicken, prawns, or your choice of protein and cook through.
  6. Season with soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, pepper, and a pinch of curry powder or chili powder.
  7. Toss in the cooked noodles and stir-fry well to combine all ingredients.
  8. Add chopped spring onions and a splash of lime juice for freshness.
  9. Stir everything well and cook for another minute or two.
  10. Serve hot, optionally garnished with fried onions or chopped coriander.
