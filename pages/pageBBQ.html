BBQ Chicken

legs,Body,Beef,Pork

2500/= Up

    Ingredients

  • 4 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken parts (legs, thighs, wings, breasts)
  • Kosher salt
  • Extra virgin olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1/2 bunch spring onion greens
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp MSG
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 chicken carcass or 4 chicken wings
  • Extra chicken fat to render (you'll need around 1/2 a cup of rendered fat in total)

    BBQ Recipe & Steps

  1. Choose your meat (chicken, beef, pork, or ribs) and trim off any excess fat.
  2. Prepare a dry rub or marinade with spices like paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and brown sugar.
  3. Apply the dry rub or marinade evenly over the meat. Let it marinate for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight in the fridge.
  4. Preheat your grill or BBQ pit to medium heat (around 250-300°F / 120-150°C).
  5. Place the meat on the grill and cook slowly, turning occasionally for even cooking.
  6. If using wood chips, add them to the coals or smoker box for a smoky flavor.
  7. Baste the meat with BBQ sauce during the last 10-15 minutes of cooking to caramelize the glaze.
  8. Cook until the meat reaches a safe internal temperature (use a meat thermometer for accuracy).
  9. Remove the meat from the grill and let it rest for 10 minutes to retain juices.
  10. Slice and serve hot with your favorite sides like coleslaw, cornbread, or grilled veggies.
