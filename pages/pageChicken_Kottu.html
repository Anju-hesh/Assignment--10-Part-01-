Kotthu Rotti

Chicken,Sea Food,Beef,Pork & etc

1200/= Up

    Ingredients

  • 1 large whole chicken (about 1.7 kg)
  • 5cm fresh unpeeled ginger, crushed
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1/2 bunch spring onion greens
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp MSG
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 chicken carcass or 4 chicken wings
  • Extra chicken fat to render (you'll need around 1/2 a cup of rendered fat in total)
  • 1 tsp salt

    • Kotthu Categery

  • Chicken Kotthu
  • Dholpin Kotthu
  • Seafood- Kotthu
  • Beef Kotthu
  • Vegiteable Kotthu
  • Egg Kotthu
  • & Etc

    Kottu Roti Recipe & Steps

  1. Prepare the roti by cutting leftover godhamba roti or any flatbread into small, thin strips.

  2. Chop onions, carrots, cabbage, green chilies, and any other vegetables finely. Prepare diced chicken, beef, or eggs for protein if desired.

  3. Heat oil or butter in a large wok or frying pan over medium-high heat.

  4. Add chopped onions and green chilies. Sauté until the onions are translucent and fragrant.

  5. Add diced chicken, beef, or scramble eggs. Cook thoroughly.

  6. Add chopped vegetables and stir-fry until they soften but still retain some crunch.

  7. Season with curry powder, turmeric, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine the flavors.

  8. Add the chopped roti pieces to the pan. Stir vigorously using spatulas or spoons to mix everything evenly and heat the roti through.

  9. Optionally, add soy sauce or a splash of lime juice for extra flavor. Mix thoroughly.

  10. Serve the kottu roti hot, optionally with spicy sambol or chutney on the side.

  11. For extra flavor, sprinkle chopped fresh curry leaves or coriander before serving.

  12. If you like it spicy, add a pinch of chili flakes or sliced green chilies while stir-frying.

  13. To add crunch, toss in some roasted cashews or fried onions just before serving.

  14. Garnish with a squeeze of fresh lime juice to brighten up the flavors right before serving.

  15. Enjoy your homemade kottu roti with a side of fresh salad or raita to balance the spices.
