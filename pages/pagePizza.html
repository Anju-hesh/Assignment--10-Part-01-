    Pizza Recipe & Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough by mixing flour, yeast, salt, sugar, olive oil, and warm water. Knead until smooth.
  2. Let the dough rise until doubled in size, about 1 to 2 hours.
  3. Roll out the dough and spread tomato sauce evenly over it.
  4. Add your favorite toppings and cheese.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven at 475°F (245°C) for 12-15 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  6. Slice and serve hot.
