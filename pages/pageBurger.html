    Burger Recipe & Steps

  1. Prepare the ground beef by seasoning it with salt, pepper, and your favorite spices.
  2. Shape the seasoned beef into patties of your desired size.
  3. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties for about 3-4 minutes on each side or until they reach your preferred doneness.
  5. Toast the burger buns lightly on the grill or in a pan.
  6. Assemble the burger by placing the cooked patty on the bottom bun.
  7. Add toppings such as lettuce, tomato slices, onions, pickles, cheese, and condiments like ketchup or mustard.
  8. Cover with the top bun.
  9. Serve immediately with your favorite sides like fries or salad.
